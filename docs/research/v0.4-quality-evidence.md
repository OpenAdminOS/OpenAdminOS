## Functional peer review

- The first exact-model Opus 5 pass found no blocker and confirmed the registry signature/digest chain, fail-closed connectors, OS-secure credential storage, non-idempotent write retry policy, and mandatory typed write confirmation.
- Its two high and three medium findings were fixed: visible disconnect failures, complete `$top` pagination, debounced Changes search, fail-fast MSAL cleanup, and end-to-end official-registry signature tests.
- Its actionable low findings were also closed: Chat cancellation recovery, bounded `Retry-After`, registry replay protection, test-file packaging exclusion without losing typechecking, schedule re-enable anchoring, and tenant chat cascade assertions.
- A focused second Opus 5 pass confirmed those closures and found two medium follow-ups. Both were fixed: item-cap paging now fails rather than truncating, and Electron host tests have a dedicated no-emit typecheck configuration.
- The new replay test exposed and closed an additional validation-order defect: a registry candidate is no longer assigned or cached until entry and revision validation succeeds.

## Findings closed in the implementation

- User-visible Chat, Settings, and write-confirmation errors sanitize stack frames, local paths, multiline exceptions, and oversized implementation details while retaining short actionable messages.
- Remembered hosted consent defaults off. Multi-tenant hosted consent records only the acknowledgement the modal actually requested.
- Write confirmation derives impact wording from the plan, distinguishes simulated from real Graph changes, and never invents a device-retirement impact for arbitrary write agents.
- Native zoom controls, informative-text contrast, global reduced motion, forced colors, focus trapping, focus return, topmost Escape, and command-palette combobox semantics are implemented and tested.
- The Chat history rail collapses and re-expands with the breakpoint without moving the primary navigation.
- Persisted conversations and Settings sections are URL-addressable. Unknown or deleted conversation URLs render a recovery state. Rail search preserves the route-owned open transcript.
- Inert `openadminos://` copy-link actions were removed until the app has a real protocol handler.
- The screenshot harness follows the persisted Chat route and captures the typed write-confirmation state.

A. First launch: the model scan

The orbit is the one bold moment of the first launch. The column beside it stays quiet: a title, one sentence, progress, and a compact check list that the screen reader announces.

OpenAdminOS
  • Sequence, about 2.4 s: device tile settles (360 ms spring), orbit and two radiating rings start, the sweep turns once every 2.2 s, nodes enter staggered 60 ms as their check starts, each node resolves with a 280 ms pop and, when found, a 420 ms link draw. Settle: sweep and rings fade in 300 ms, not-found nodes shrink to 32 px and drop their status line, the result column rises in 60 ms steps.
  • Skip scan, a click on the stage, or any key settles at once. Reduced motion: no rings, sweep, scale or draw; nodes and the result appear in their final state, progress text still updates.
  • Labels sit on a background plate so the orbit line never crosses text. Not-found labels use text-muted, never opacity.

B. First launch: choose what answers

One question, one filled action. Models on this device come first and the first one that can answer is preselected; signed-in tools are listed with where prompts go and are never preselected. Picking a row highlights its link in the orbit; picking a node selects the row.

OpenAdminOS
  • Outcome titles: one local model ready, "OpenAdmin 8B is ready on this device" with Open Chat. Several options, as here, "Choose what answers in Chat". Nothing found, "No model on this device yet" with the Ollama command and Show all providers.
  • The action label follows the selection: "Start Ollama and open Chat", "Start LM Studio and open Chat", "Use Claude Code", "Use Codex". Starting shows a 16 px spinner in the button and keeps the label; a failed start puts the reason under the row, not in a banner.

## Neutral marks instead of logos

These providers show a neutral two-letter mark with their name, because their guidelines do not allow a third party to show the logo in a product without the owner's prior permission:

- **Claude Code (Anthropic):** https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines ("only use our trademarks as specifically permitted by us and only in materials we approve beforehand").
- **Codex (OpenAI):** https://openai.com/brand/ (use of the logo needs OpenAI's permission; requests go to partnercomms@openai.com).
- **Gemini CLI (Google):** https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/trademarks-and-terms/terms-and-conditions/ (Google brand features only after Google expressly approves a request).
- **Azure OpenAI (Microsoft):** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/icons/ (icons only in architectural diagrams, training materials or documentation).
- **Apple Intelligence (Apple):** Apple's trademark guidelines do not allow third parties to use the Apple logo in their products.

Guidelines checked on 2026-11-09.
